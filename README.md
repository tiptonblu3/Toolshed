Controls (Mouse & Keyboard):

W or Up Arrow Key - Move Up

A or Left Arrow Key - Move Left

S or Down Arrow Key - Move Down

D or Right Arrow Key - Move Right

Left Click - Throw Football, Select

Escape - Quit Game

---

Controls (Controller):

Joystick - Movement

X / A - Select

Right Trigger - Throw Football

---

Features & Mechanics: 

KnightDug features 10 levels to dig through. In each, you will have to face Knightro's enemies, Citronaught and Puff the Dragon.

You begin with 3 lives; once those lives run out, you have to begin again at Level 1. Your lives are displayed on the top right.

On the top left are your points. You gain points by killing enemies. Your high score is displayed when you lose all your lives as well.

Both enemy types will attempt to kill you by running into you. Puff the Dragon has the extra ability to shoot a blob of bubbles at you. Both enemies can also enter a 'ghost mode' where they will phase through the ground to reach you.

You must defend yourself by throwing footballs at the enemies. All enemies die in one hit to a football, giving you 100 points. The bubbles can also be destroyed by footballs, granting you 100 points as well.

Try to get the highest score you can across the 10 levels!
